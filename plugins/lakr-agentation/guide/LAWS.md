## What the best launch films do (Lakr's study of 2,024 launch films)
- LENGTH: 20–35 s is the sweet spot; likes per view halve past 50 s. Never pad.
- OPENING: frame 0 is the film's ground with at most one element; something moves by frame 6; product or its one-line purpose legible by 1.0 s. ONE element on a calm ground — a UI atom being clicked, or one small sentence typing in. Never a busy wall, a montage or a logo sting first.
- SOUND-OFF: every claim must exist as on-screen text; sound is a bonus layer. Claims sit on screen as tiny centred sentences (≈2–3 % of the frame height) or one huge word (10–25 %) — never mid-size captions.
- SPACE: keep 80-90% of the pixels on the ground colour in product styles (premium styles measure 11-21% off-ground content); about a third of frames nearly empty (one word or one atom); never fill the frame with a whole desktop. Colour is a point (one word, one pill, one ring), not a field.
- CUTS: almost none — at most 0.17 per second; scenes join by widening, growing or cutting into and out of a sentence card.
- ENDING: mark or wordmark alone on the film's ground (or black for dark styles), 1-3 s, optional one small line; arrives by a small rise + unblur, never a spin.
- SOUND: music at -14 LUFS, true peak -1 dBTP; soft ticks on real clicks; whooshes only where something actually moves.
- NEVER: the capture inset on a gradient or wallpaper frame, no type, often silent; captions at 5-8% of height in calm films; a saturated full-frame ground for the whole film; a wall of UI or a montage in the first second.
